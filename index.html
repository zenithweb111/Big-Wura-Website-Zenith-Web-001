Our Story

Beauty starts from healthy hair.

Big Wura Hair Care began in early 2026 with one simple belief: that beauty starts from healthy hair. Our founder built this business to become financially independent while helping people around her appreciate what properly cared-for hair can do for how they feel.

Whether you wear your hair natural or love a good wig install, we believe every strand deserves attention. That's why we source and stock only products we'd confidently use ourselves — shampoos, conditioners, tools, and accessories that actually do what they promise.

What started small has grown into a trusted name for students, young professionals, and hair lovers across Lagos and Ogun State — one satisfied customer, and one referral, at a time.
